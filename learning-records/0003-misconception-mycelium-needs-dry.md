# Misconception: "mycelium needs dry soil"

User said the mycelium needs dry soil while mushrooms need high humidity and fresh air. Correct: the fruiting half is right; the colonizing mycelium needs a *moist* substrate (hyphae absorb food dissolved in water) and tolerates stale, CO₂-rich air, which is why it works buried in logs or bags. "Dry" is wrong, and "soil" is just one possible food source.

**Implications**: next lesson should contrast colonization vs fruiting conditions (moisture in the substrate vs humid, fresh, open air) and why the switch triggers pinning. Source: [Cornell indoor production](https://smallfarms.cornell.edu/projects/mushrooms/indoor-production/). Verify the exact wording against the lesson before teaching it.
