# Can state the basic mushroom life cycle

User restated it unprompted: spore → hyphae → mycelium → mushroom → spores. Two refinements given: mycelium grows in any food source (wood, leaf litter, grain), not just soil; and in mushroom-forming fungi, two compatible mycelia must fuse before a mushroom can form ([OpenStax §24.2](https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/24-2-classifications-of-fungi)).

**Implications**: ready for the mating/fusion step, or for substrate and colonization (the bridge to home growing). Watch the word "spawn": the user used it to mean "produce", but in cultivation it means colonized starter material.
