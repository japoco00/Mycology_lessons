Keep these distinctions straight

Hyphae are the fine filaments that grow through or across a food source. A mass or network of hyphae is a mycelium, the vegetative body of many fungi.

A fruiting body is a reproductive structure made by some fungi. The familiar mushroom is not the whole organism; it is the conspicuous structure that produces spores.

Not every fungus makes a mushroom. Yeasts, for example, are single-celled fungi, and many fungi form other kinds of reproductive structures.
