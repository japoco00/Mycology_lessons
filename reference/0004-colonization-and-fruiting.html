Colonization & fruiting

The two phases between adding spawn and harvesting mushrooms. From Lesson 4.

Two phases, opposite conditions

PhaseWhat the fungus is doingAir (CO₂) & lightHumidity & temp
1. Colonization (spawn run)Claiming substrate, digesting food, fighting off microbesHigh CO₂ tolerated; darkness fineMoisture locked in substrate; steady warmth
2. Pinning & fruitingKnotting hyphae into primordia and inflating mushroomsFresh air (CO₂ drops); indirect light for directionHigh air humidity (85–95%); often a mild cool-down

Why competitors lose (or win)

Pre-treatment: Pasteurization uses mild heat to knock back rival moulds and bacteria while leaving heat-tolerant helpers; sterilization wipes everything out.

Fungal defence: Established mycelium holds territory physically and chemically, releasing secondary metabolites such as antibiotics (e.g. penicillin) to suppress bacteria.
