Mushroom life cycle (gilled mushrooms)

How a club fungus (Basidiomycota) gets from spore to mushroom. From Lesson 3.

StageWhat's in each cellCan it fruit?
SporeOne nucleus—
Primary mycelium (one parent)One nucleusNo—grows and feeds only
Secondary mycelium (after two compatible mating types fuse)Two separate nuclei, one per parentYes—main, long-lived stage
Basidium (on the gills)The two nuclei fuse, genes reshuffleMakes 4 new spores

Other designs

Sac fungi (truffles, morels, yeasts): spores form inside sacs, not on gills. Many moulds: mostly copy themselves with asexual spores.

For growers

Start from spawn (established mycelium on grain), not raw spores: faster and less contamination.

Italian

gills → lamelle · basidium → basidio · truffle → tartufo · morel → spugnola
